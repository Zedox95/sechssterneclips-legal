Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

"Sechs Sterne Clips" is a private tool used only by its operator for the operator's own TikTok account @sechssterneclips. It has no other users and does not collect data from visitors.

Data we process

  • The operator's TikTok authorization token (via TikTok Login Kit), used only to upload videos and read view counts of the operator's own videos.
  • Video files, titles and view statistics of the operator's own account.

Storage and sharing

The token is stored on a private server controlled by the operator, with restricted file permissions. Data is never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Deletion

The operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions, and delete the stored token.

Contact
